Brand guidelines · v1.0 · October 2026

Trafix brand

Trafix is the paid-traffic app that measures Meta and Google ads in profit, not vanity ROAS.

Logo

The Trafix mark is a single lime light-trail that bends sharply once: traffic that has been corrected onto the profitable line. Download: logo.svg · logo-dark.svg · mark.svg

  • Keep clear space equal to the height of the mark on every side.
  • Minimum size: 24 px tall on screen. Use the mark alone below 120 px wide.
  • Never recolour, stretch, outline or add effects. Use the dark version on #0E0E10 and photography.
  • Pair with the SOMIN partner lockup only as “Technology partner SOMIN”, never merged into the Trafix logo.

Colour

Accent is for actions, highlights and data. Body text on light surfaces uses #2A2B2E; links use #4D6B00 to keep AA contrast.

Section label

See what your Meta and Google ads really earn

Trafix replaces vanity ROAS with true-profit ROAS, net of returns, fees and discounts. Then it tells you which creatives are tiring, where budget should move, and which new ads are worth paying to test.

Personality

  • Candid
  • Precise
  • Fast
  • Commercially minded

Voice: do

  • Lead with the profit number and the decision it implies
  • Show the working behind every recommendation
  • Use plain finance language a CFO would accept

Voice: don't

  • Celebrate platform ROAS without netting costs
  • Promise automated growth or guaranteed returns
  • Hide assumptions inside a single score

Imagery

Electric lime light-trails and flows on matte black, long-exposure speed and motion, abstract data rather than people or screenshots. Never text inside images.

All imagery is generated with SOMIN image generation from art-directed prompts: no stock, no text baked into images, no identifiable real people.
